# Kivo Features

**AI Tutor**
Ask any question about your textbook and get accurate, streamed answers grounded in verified content — no hallucination.

**Homework Companion**
Get guided help on homework problems through a Socratic approach — the AI nudges you toward the answer rather than just giving it away.

**Interactive Quizzes**
Practice with AI-generated MCQs, short-answer, and long-answer questions. Answers are graded automatically, with hints available and per-question review.

**Practice Papers**
Generate full-length exam papers from a curated question bank and get them graded with per-question breakdowns and total scores.

**Interactive Reader**
Read your textbook inside the app — select any text to instantly get definitions, explanations, or deeper context. Includes chapter exercises and discovery guides.

**Kivo Docs**
AI-powered writing companion for school assignments, grounded in your textbook curriculum.
- **Writing Suggestions** — Flags scientific vocabulary gaps, missing concepts, misconceptions, and clarity issues as you write.
- **Paragraph Coach** — Select any paragraph for feedback on what works, what could improve, and a suggested rewrite that preserves the student's own voice.
- **Guided Response Builder** — Enter an assignment topic and get a structured document scaffold (headings + writing prompts) pulled from relevant textbook chapters.

**Progress Tracking**
See your activity at a glance: chat count, quiz accuracy, paper scores, and your daily study streak.

**Textbook Library**
Browse and access all your subscribed textbooks, view tables of contents, and navigate to chapter-specific materials.

**Feedback**
Rate your experience and send the team comments directly from the app.

**Subscriptions & Payments**
Purchase annual subscriptions with coupon support (via Razorpay), with a 15-day refund and change window.
